1. Multi-oracle aggregation must not allow manipulation of the easiest-to-influence source to determine the final price.
2. The share price seen by depositors and withdrawers must not be manipulable by sandwiching internal swaps.
3. Pool rate functions must not be readable in an inconsistent state during pool operations that trigger ETH callbacks.
4. Price used in rebalance operations must not be manipulable within a single transaction.
5. Oracle price sources must not be alterable within the same transaction that consumes the price for financial decisions.
6. The virtual price used for collateral valuation must not be readable in an inconsistent state during pool operations.
7. Symmetric operations (mint/redeem) must use the same price source to prevent arbitrage from price discrepancies.
8. The TWAP observation window must be long enough that the cost of sustaining price manipulation exceeds the potential profit
   from exploiting the manipulated price.
9. The cost of manipulating the price oracle must exceed the maximum extractable value from the manipulation.
10. LP token valuation must not be inflatable by manipulating pool reserves within a single transaction.
11. Correlated asset pricing must account for potential depegs by using the specific asset's oracle feed, not the underlying's
   feed.
12. Price used for collateral valuation and liquidation must not be manipulable within a single transaction.
13. Collateral valuation must produce correct USD values regardless of the deposited token's decimal precision.
14. Oracle prices used for PnL calculations must be current within the feed's heartbeat interval.
15. Each accepted oracle price update must have a publishTime strictly greater than the previously accepted update.
16. The price oracle used for collateral valuation must not be manipulable within a single atomic transaction.
17. Reward distribution must be proportional to the duration of stake, not just the point-in-time balance at distribution.
18. Every swap must enforce a minimum output amount that reflects the fair market exchange rate minus an acceptable slippage
    tolerance.
19. A TWAP must weight each price observation by its time duration, not count each observation equally.
20. Asset valuation must use a manipulation-resistant price source, not a DEX quote function that reflects current pool state.
21. No depositor should lose assets due to share price inflation caused by a prior deposit plus donation sequence.
